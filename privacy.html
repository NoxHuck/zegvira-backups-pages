Privacy policy

Effective date: October 7, 2026.

Zegvira Backups is a private utility operated by the account owner to back up the owner's project. Google Drive authorization is used to locate the backup folder, upload encrypted archives, verify copies, and remove older backup archives according to the owner's retention settings.

The Drive permission requested by the utility allows access to Drive files. The utility is configured to operate on the backup folder; it is not intended to process unrelated Drive files.

Archives are encrypted before upload. The private decryption key remains with the owner. Google authorization tokens and client settings are kept in a restricted configuration directory on the owner's server and are excluded from backup archives.

Google user data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with other users. Backup archives are stored in the owner's Google Drive. The utility does not use Drive data to train artificial intelligence models.

The owner can revoke access in Google Account settings and delete stored archives from Google Drive. Removing authorization does not automatically delete existing archives.

This page has no analytics, tracking scripts, or forms. Hosting providers may process ordinary connection information to serve it. This utility is for personal use; it does not offer a public account registration or support service.
